# Ignidex Philosophy

Ignidex is a small, local-first start page.

Its purpose is to provide a simple place to:

* open links
* trigger small actions
* copy useful text
* keep short checklists

Nothing more is required.

---

## Scope and Intent

Ignidex focuses on helping users **get started quickly**.
It is intentionally limited in scope and avoids becoming a general-purpose dashboard or productivity system.

Features that introduce significant complexity, automation, or ongoing attention requirements are considered out of scope.

---

## Simplicity by Design

Ignidex aims to be simple and predictable.

This means:

* few concepts
* clear behavior
* minimal configuration
* no unnecessary abstraction

A tool that works quietly and reliably is preferred over one that is feature-rich but demanding.

---

## Local and Privacy-First

Ignidex works without accounts, tracking, or external services by default.

Data is stored locally.
Sharing is explicit.
Remote content is treated as read-only.

These constraints are intentional and guide design decisions.

---

## Stability Over Growth

Ignidex values stability and long-term usability over rapid expansion.

A feature-complete, low-maintenance state is considered a valid and successful outcome.

---

## Guiding Principle

When evaluating changes, preference should be given to options that:

* reduce complexity
* preserve clarity
* minimize maintenance
* keep the tool easy to use and easy to ignore

Ignidex is meant to support daily work without becoming the focus of it.
